03 / RESEARCH

A dataset designed around what could have happened.

The interesting work is not the model name. It is preserving the correct research population, enforcing decision-time causality, and preventing overlapping future information from leaking across evaluation boundaries.

01Preserve rejected opportunitiesPopulation integrity

CandidateEvent records every raw Strategy 8887 opportunity, including candidates blocked by cash, session, timing, or concurrency gates. Training only on executed trades would silently remove parts of the opportunity population.

02Isolate candidate outcomesCounterfactual labels

CandidateOutcome evaluates each opportunity independently on later bars. It is explicitly not a portfolio result and excludes portfolio-level effects. That keeps hypothetical opportunity returns separate from actual simulated execution.

03Freeze causal featuresDecision-time information

The model vector uses values available on the completed candidate bar. Outcome fields, identity metadata, portfolio state, and future-derived information are kept outside the estimator input.

04Validate chronologicallyNo random split

Financial observations are time-dependent. Quanta assigns fixed calendar windows so training always precedes evaluation; random splitting is structurally unavailable in the validation module.

05Purge and embargo overlapsLeakage control

Each label consumes an information interval from candidate time through outcome resolution. Overlapping training intervals are purged, then a fixed 40-minute embargo excludes observations immediately after the evaluation boundary.

06Define TAKE / SKIP narrowlyMeta-labeling

The target asks whether an isolated candidate return exceeds a frozen gross-return hurdle. It does not predict direction, size a position, or replace deterministic strategy and risk logic. No fitted production model is claimed.

  2. 02

    Backtests are treated as versioned experiments with stored metrics, configuration fingerprints, and failure-visible lifecycle states.

    REPRODUCIBILITY
  3. 03

    Immutable candidate contracts

    Frozen event and outcome records keep decision-time facts, historical strategy metadata, future labels, and provenance in separate roles.

    DATA CONTRACTS
  4. 04

    Blocked raw candidates are preserved and evaluated independently, making the research population larger and more honest than executed trades alone.

    POPULATION BIAS
  5. 05

    VALIDATION
